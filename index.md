# Hello, I'm Allison Li
Welcome to my mechanical engineering portfolio. I'm a student passionate about CAD, mechanical design, and bringing digital design concepts to life.

## Featured Projects
* **[Completed] Gel Pen Assembly (AutoCAD)**
  * Designed a multi-component gel pen meeting strict geometric and assembly constraints.
  * Generated 2D manufacturing drawings and individual part models, complete with standardized title blocks.
  * *Software used:* AutoCAD

* **[In Progress] Crank-Powered Dragon Automata Mechanism**
  * Developing a complex mechanical automata featuring a custom gear and linkage system.
  * Planning a hybrid manufacturing approach utilizing 3D printed components and laser-cut wooden structural/gear elements.
  * *Software used:* SolidWorks / Hand Sketching

* **[In Progress] Rover Claw Design**
  * Conceptualizing a robotic claw able to grasp and perform precise manipulations, such as inserting a USB into the port.
  * Currently in the design and prototyping phase, and researching how to incorporate PLA and TPU filaments to optimize grasp strength and flexibility.
  * *Software used:* SolidWorks

* **[Completed] First Print Trophy (Fusion 360)**
  * A milestone project commemorating my introduction to additive manufacturing. 
  * Features custom text embossing, fillet optimization, and slicer configuration.
  * *Software used:* Fusion 360, PrusaSlicer, 3D Printing (PLA)
